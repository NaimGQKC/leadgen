Kanuk winter coats are engineered for extreme cold, typically featuring temperature ratings from 0°C to -35°C. The brand utilizes high-performance insulation technologies like Thermo+ synthetic and RDS-certified duck down to maintain warmth in humid urban environments.

Core Technical Specifications & Materials

Kanuk coats are distinguished by their "architectured" design, specifically adapted for the Quebec climate.

Insulation Types:
Thermo+ Synthetic: A proprietary continuous fibre network that maintains loft and integrity even in damp conditions.
Natural Down: A 90/10 duck down and feather blend with up to 800 fill power, certified by the Responsible Down Standard (RDS).
Thindown: A patented down fabric that provides warmth without the traditional bulk of a puffer.

Shell Materials:
Mini-Ottoman: 100% recycled polyester with a microporous hydrophobic lamination and DWR (Durable Water Repellent) treatment.
Vintage Ripstop: 100% nylon used in signature heritage models for durability.

Key Features:
Weatherproofing: Most models offer 10,000 mm waterproofing and are fully windproof.
Closures: Two-way heavy-duty zippers often protected by snap-closure storm flaps.
Cuffs: Inset Lycra or wool-blend rib-knit storm cuffs to seal out drafts.

Care and Maintenance

To preserve the technical properties of the materials, Kanuk recommends:
Washing: Use a front-loading machine only; top-loaders can damage the water-repellent shell.
Detergent: Use bleach-free detergent specifically formulated for down or synthetics.
Drying: Tumble dry on medium heat with drying balls to restore loft. Do not dry clean unless specified (some leather/wool trims require specialist care).
