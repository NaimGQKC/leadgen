Query: best heritage winter coat brand Quebec Montreal

For heritage winter coats in Quebec, Kanuk and Quartz Co. are widely considered the gold standards due to their long histories and continued manufacturing within Montreal.

Top Heritage Brands from Quebec

Kanuk: Founded in 1970, Kanuk is the quintessential Montreal heritage brand. For over 50 years, they have handcrafted parkas in their Plateau-area workshop. Their coats are famous for being specifically engineered for Quebec's humid, bone-chilling winters and often feature their iconic Snowy Owl logo.

Quartz Co.: Known for its "Heritage" collection, Quartz Co. has been operating since 1997. They maintain a Montreal factory where they produce high-performance down coats that blend a minimalist, timeless aesthetic with extreme cold protection.

Audvik: Operating for over 40 years, Audvik designs and handcrafts coats in their Chabanel-district workshop in Montreal. They are a go-to for those seeking local artisanal production with a focus on sustainability and recycled materials.

Bilodeau Canada: Based in Normandin, Quebec, Bilodeau is a more traditional heritage brand known for its deep roots in northern craftsmanship and the use of natural materials, creating rugged gear meant for the harshest Canadian environments.
