Arc'teryx jackets are categorized by series (Beta, Alpha, Rush, Sabre) and sub-types (SV, AR, SL), each utilizing specific GORE-TEX membranes and proprietary materials for varying alpine conditions. For the 2026 season, pricing for flagship shells generally ranges from $400 CAD to $1,100 CAD.

Core Jacket Technologies

GORE-TEX PRO ePE: A new, lighter, and PFAS-free membrane that provides high-performing waterproof-breathable protection.
Coreloft™ Insulation: A synthetic polyester staple fibre used in insulated models (like the Beta Insulated) to provide warmth even when wet.
Hadron™ Face Fabric: A lightweight yet highly abrasion-resistant fabric pairing (e.g., in the Alpha Jacket) designed for technical ascents.
C-KNIT™ Backer: A soft, quiet lining technology that improves next-to-skin comfort and breathability compared to traditional crinkly hardshells.
RECCO® Reflector: A passive search-and-rescue device embedded in the hood or brim of most technical shells.
