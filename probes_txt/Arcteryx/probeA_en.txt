Where to Buy in Montreal

MEC (Mountain Equipment Company): Located near Crémazie metro and in Laval, they carry their own Gore-Tex line (the Synergy) alongside brands like Patagonia and Arc'teryx.

La Cordée: A local staple with multiple locations, including a major boutique at Place Alexis Nihon and on Sainte-Catherine O.

Altitude Sports: While primarily an online retailer based in Montreal, they often offer the widest selection of technical colors and sizes with fast local delivery.

SAIL: Large stores in Brossard and Laval that stock a wide range of Rab, Marmot, and Mountain Hardwear technical gear.

Montreal Weather Tip
A technical Gore-Tex shell is just a "hardshell"—it provides no warmth on its own. To survive a Montreal winter, you must pair it with a "mid-layer" like the Arc'teryx Atom Hoodie or a down vest.
