Montreal's boot scene is a blend of high-fashion European imports and rugged, locally designed heritage brands that handle the city's slush with style. You can find everything from handmade Italian leather at Maguire Shoes to the iconic, winter-ready designs of Pajar Canada.

Top Local Designers & Specialized Boutiques

These stores are celebrated for their unique selections and high-quality craftsmanship, often designed right in Montreal.

Maguire Shoes: Founded by sisters Myriam and Romy, this brand focuses on making high-end European styles accessible. Their Lucca Leather Knee-High Boots are a standout for a sleek, "moto" look without sacrificing quality.

La Canadienne: Known for their "City Dry" technology, these boots are specifically designed for Montreal weather—waterproof yet dressy enough for the office or dinner. Popular models like the Cye Pebbled Leather Bootie offer a classic Chelsea style with weather protection.

Pajar Canada: A Montreal institution since 1963, Pajar combines heritage with innovation. Their Bili men's boot, for example, is designed for temperatures as low as -40°C.

Chaussures Sandrini Shoes: Located in Old Montreal, this family-owned shop is highly rated for its curated selection of European brands and unique styles you won't find in major chains.

Boutique Tozzi: A go-to for trendy, high-end men's and women's footwear in a minimalist setting on Crescent Street. They are praised for carrying unique, "secret pleasure" pieces.

Stylish Boot Recommendations

Whether you need a rugged everyday boot or something for a night out, these options are currently popular in Montreal:

Chelsea & Ankle Boots:
Blundstone 558 Classic: A city-wide favorite for its "style and utility" and ease of use. Available at local shops like Mile End Kicks.
Vagabond Hedda: A minimalist, 70s-inspired leather boot with a flared heel, available at La Maison Simons.

Heritage & Rugged Boots:
Thursday Boot Co. Diplomat: A streamlined moc-toe boot that ages well and is versatile for both casual and dressy outfits.
La Canadienne Conquer: A shearling-lined combat boot that's perfect for Montreal's snow.

Shopping Tips for Montreal

Old Montreal (Vieux-Port): Visit for unique boutiques like Felix Boutique (sneakers and espresso) or Sandrini.
Mile-End & Plateau: These neighborhoods are the heart of local design; Maguire Shoes is located on Boul. Saint-Laurent.
Sales Events: Look out for La Grande Braderie de Mode Québécoise, where local designers sell surplus stock and samples at deep discounts.
