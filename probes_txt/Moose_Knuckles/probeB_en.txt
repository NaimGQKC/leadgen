Moose Knuckles jackets are defined by high-performance technical materials designed for extreme Canadian winters, typically featuring a signature 74% cotton and 26% nylon blend and heavy-duty insulation.

Core Technical Specifications & Materials

The brand's "Originals" and "Classic Core" collections use a standardized set of high-end components:

Outer Shell: Most core models use a durable 74% Cotton / 26% Nylon blend, though newer technical lines like the Laki or Greystone may use a slightly stretchy 82% Nylon / 18% Elastane shell.
Weatherproofing: Shells are typically treated with a Durable Water Repellent (DWR) coating and a waterproof laminated backing to shield against wind and water in temperatures as low as -40°C.
Insulation (Down): Uses ethically sourced, RDS-Certified duck or goose down. Fill powers typically range from 650 to 800+ with a standard 90/10 down-to-feather ratio for maximum loft and warmth.

Trims & Hardware:
Fur: Premium models feature Blue Fox fur or Sheepskin/Shearling trims.
Logo: Iconic metal "MK" sleeve logos are made from various metals, including gunmetal, antique nickel, and 14k gold plating.
Zippers: Heavy-duty, industrial-strength YKK metal zippers (often 2-way).
Lining: Often uses 100% down-proof nylon or polyester designed to be tear and snag-proof.
