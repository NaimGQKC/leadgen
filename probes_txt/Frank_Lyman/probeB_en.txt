Frank Lyman is a Canadian fashion brand known for its sophisticated women's clothing that emphasizes high-quality materials and a flattering, inclusive fit. Most of the collection is Made in Canada, with approximately 98% of production occurring within a 30-kilometre radius of their Montreal headquarters.

Material Specifications & Fabric Technology

The brand focuses on luxurious, easy-care fabrics that offer both comfort and a refined aesthetic. Key material characteristics include:

Stretch & Mobility: Most garments use high-quality knit fabrics typically composed of Polyester (92-95%) blended with Elastane/Spandex (5-8%) to ensure a flexible, "forgiving" fit that moves with the body.

Fabric Variety: Collections frequently feature specialized materials like chiffon, silk, lace, and mesh. Some items incorporate metallic fibres (around 7%) for subtle shimmer effects.

Easy Care: Many pieces are machine washable or require minimal maintenance, designed for the modern woman's lifestyle.
