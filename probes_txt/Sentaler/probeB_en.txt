Sentaler coats are primarily engineered from premium Peruvian alpaca fibres that feature microscopic air pockets acting as natural thermal insulators, providing exceptional warmth while remaining remarkably lightweight. Pricing for core outerwear styles generally ranges from $1,850 CAD to $3,450 CAD, depending on the length and the rarity of the specific alpaca blend.

Material Specifications & Technology

The brand utilizes three main categories of ethical, cruelty-free alpaca fibres, each with distinct technical properties:

Baby Alpaca: Sourced from the first shearing, these 20-micron fibres are exceptionally soft, hypoallergenic, and highly durable. Most iconic wrap coats use a blend of 70% baby alpaca and 30% wool.

Suri Alpaca: A rarer breed known for longer, silkier hair with a high natural sheen. These pieces often feature the Zibeline technique.

Bouclé Alpaca: A textured, looped fibre that creates a plush, thick garment.

Performance Features: Alpaca is naturally water-resistant and resilient, resisting matting, pilling, and stains more effectively than standard wool.

Technical Design Elements

Signature Ribbed Detail: A hallmark brand element typically found on sleeve cuffs or upper arms, providing structural definition.

Natural Insulation: The fiber technology allows for high thermal performance without the bulk of traditional overcoats.

Buttons: Often features signature Corozo buttons, which are durable, sustainable, and logo-engraved.

Care: Professional dry cleaning is recommended every few seasons; the brand explicitly advises not to steam the delicate fibres.
