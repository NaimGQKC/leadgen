Query: Sentaler vs Max Mara luxury coat comparison

Choosing between Sentaler and Max Mara depends on whether you prefer modern, functional luxury or heritage Italian elegance. Sentaler is renowned for its alpaca wool and signature ribbed cuffs, while Max Mara is the gold standard for iconic wool and cashmere silhouettes like the 101801.

Quick Comparison

Feature | Sentaler | Max Mara
Signature Material | Alpaca Wool (70% Baby Alpaca, 30% Wool) | Camel Hair, Virgin Wool, and Cashmere
Key Design | Ribbed cuffs, oversized shawl collars, wrap styles | Oversized "kimono" sleeves, double-breasted silhouettes
Warmth & Weight | Lightweight with thermal air pockets; thinner than cashmere but very warm | Dense, luxurious feel; options range from unlined to heavy "Teddy" styles
Price Range | $1,500 – $4,000+ CAD | $1,000 – $8,000+ CAD (varies by sub-brand)
Vibe | Modern, "the Canadian Max Mara," favored by royalty | Timeless, "investment pieces," epitome of high-end Italian tailoring

Sentaler: The Modern Luxury Choice

Alpaca fibre contains microscopic air pockets for insulation.
Unique Aesthetic: The most recognizable feature is the ribbed sleeve detail, which adds a modern, architectural touch to classic wrap coats.
Fit & Feel: Often described as thinner than traditional cashmere but still high-quality. Their Long Wide-Collar Wrap Coat is a staple for those seeking a cinched, feminine silhouette.
Maintenance: Alpaca wool has a more textured feel and is less prone to the "slick" finish of fine cashmere, though it still requires professional care.

Max Mara: The Timeless Icon

The Icons:
101801 Madame: The most famous coat, featuring perfect oversized proportions and a wool/cashmere blend.
Manuela: A "forever coat" made of 100% camel wool.
Teddy Bear Coat: A plush, oversized style made from alpaca, wool, and silk.
Sub-Brands: You can find more accessible entry points through S'Max Mara (often unlined).

Where to Buy

Sentaler at sentaler.com or retailers like Saks Fifth Avenue. Max Mara can be found at maxmara.com and luxury department stores like Simons.
