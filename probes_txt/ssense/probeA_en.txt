Montreal offers a blend of world-class luxury department stores, specialized high-end boutiques, and newly developed flagship destinations.

Premier Luxury Destinations

Holt Renfrew Ogilvy | Détaillant en mode designer, beauté et style de vie du Canada
4.2 (1.3K) - Department store
Closed - 1307 Rue Sainte-Catherine O - 350.0 m
The city's definitive luxury department store, featuring 23,000 square metres of high-end fashion, beauty, and accessories. It houses exclusive "shop-in-shop" boutiques for world-renowned brands like Chanel, Louis Vuitton, Hermès, Prada, and Saint Laurent.

ROYALMOUNT
4.2 (5.3K) - Shopping mall
Closed - Mount Royal, Quebec - 7.3 km
A massive, newly opened luxury lifestyle destination that boasts the highest concentration of international flagship stores in Quebec. It features standalone boutiques for brands such as Gucci, Versace, Jimmy Choo, Moncler, and Longchamp.

SSENSE Montréal
3.3 (1K) - Clothing store
Closed - 418 Rue Saint-Sulpice - 1.6 km
Located in a historic building in Old Montreal, this five-storey space is the physical flagship of the globally recognized online retailer. It is a hub for avant-garde and cutting-edge designer pieces from brands like Balmain, Margiela, and Charlotte Olympia.
