Nicole Benisti coats are luxury Canadian outerwear known for blending high-fashion "mixed-media" aesthetics—such as tweed, leather, and shearling—with high-performance winter technology.

Core Technical Specifications & Materials

The brand's construction typically features a multi-layered approach to maximize warmth and durability:

Insulation & Down Power: Most premium puffers utilize a 90/10 goose down-to-feather ratio for optimum insulation. Specific performance models, like the Chelsea Parka, are rated at 650 fill power.
Shell Technology: Shells are often crafted from water-repellent and wind-resistant mega-twill or nylon.
Luxury Mixed-Media: Signature designs incorporate French imported tweed, Italian leather accents, and genuine shearling (lamb or sheep).
Temperature Rating: Many models are designed for city winters, with some heavier parkas suitable for temperatures down to approximately -20°C to -25°C.

Care Instructions

Due to the delicate nature of the mixed-media (leather, fur, and down), all Nicole Benisti coats require professional dry clean only. It is recommended to store them in a spacious closet away from direct light and heat to prevent leather and fur from drying out or fading.
