La Canadienne boots are engineered for urban performance, combining Italian-sourced materials with Canadian weatherproofing expertise. Their footwear is primarily defined by the City Dry™ technology, which provides complete water protection without sacrificing style.

Core Technology & Innovation

City Dry™ Technology: Signature waterproofing where Italian leather and suede are treated throughout the material, not just on the surface. This allows for immediate wear in wet conditions without initial treatment.

Breathable Design: All components, including waterproof membranes and linings, are designed to permit moisture evaporation, keeping feet both warm and dry from morning to night.

Advanced Traction Soles:
- OCSystem Ice-Grip: Features rotating flip-grips that switch between built-in snow cleats (GRIP-ON) and flat surfaces (GRIP-OFF) for ice safety.
- Vibram Arctic Grip: A specialized rubber compound for superior traction on wet ice.

Materials & Construction

Leather: Primarily Italian waterproof leather and suede, known for a smooth feel and durability.

Insoles: Cushioned memory foam is standard across most models, often accompanied by removable OrthoLite inserts for enhanced breathability.

Manufacturing & Craftsmanship

Origin: Most boots are made in Italy using expert craftsmanship. Some specialty styles are handmade in Spain.

Local Heritage: The brand maintains a factory in Montreal, which has produced the City Dry™ collection for over 35 years.
