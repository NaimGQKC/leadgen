Query: best affordable fashion coat Montreal

For an affordable fashion coat in Montreal, your best bets are local department stores like Simons, specialized clearance events like La Grande Braderie, or exploring the city's thriving vintage and thrift scene.

Top Stores for Affordable Fashion Coats

Simons
A Montreal staple, Simons offers a vast selection of stylish coats ranging from their affordable in-house brands like Twik and Icône to mid-range designer labels. Shoppers often find significant deals, sometimes up to 75% off during seasonal sales.

Créations GAMA - Vieux-Port - Dubarry Fourrures
Located in the Old Port, this second-generation manufacturer is highly rated for providing high-quality wool, alpaca, and shearling coats at "very reasonable" and "excellent" prices.

Liquidation Traf
A well-known liquidation store offering deeply discounted brand-name fashion coats.

Kanuk
While Kanuk is a premium Montreal brand, their outlet on Rachel Street offers heavy-duty, stylish winter coats at approximately 50% off retail price.

Local Fashion Events & Thrifting

La Grande Braderie de Mode Québécoise: This massive "sample sale" event occurs in the spring and autumn (next scheduled for October 22, 2026 at Bonsecours Market) and offers up to 80% off on Quebec-designed fashion, including coats. Brands like Kanuk, Quartz Co, and others regularly participate, offering significant markdown from their usual $475+ retail price.

Eva B: A world-famous vintage store on St-Laurent Blvd where you can find unique jackets and coats for $30–$40. It offers two floors of highly affordable, curated vintage fashion.

Plateau & Mile End Thrifting: Neighborhoods like the Plateau and Mile End are hubs for ethical and affordable fashion. Consider a Secondhand Shopping Tour to find hidden gems in high-end vintage and budget-friendly shops.
