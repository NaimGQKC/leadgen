For a Montreal winter, the best leather jacket must combine a wind-resistant leather shell with high-performance insulation like down or shearling to handle temperatures that can drop to -30°C.

Key Features for Montreal Winters

Insulation Type: Look for Sheepskin/Shearling for natural breathability and warmth, or Down-filled leather for the highest heat retention in deep freezes.
Leather Material: Sheepskin and Lambskin are softer and provide better natural insulation compared to cowhide, which can become stiff in extreme cold.
Local Services: Brands like Cuir Dimitri offer specialized local services including waterproofing, organic cleaning, and alterations, which are essential for maintaining leather against Montreal's road salt and slush.
