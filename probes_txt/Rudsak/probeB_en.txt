Rudsak leather jackets typically range in price from $595 CAD to over $2,000 CAD, depending on the complexity of the design and the specific materials used, such as shearling or down insulation. Crafted primarily from premium, supple lamb and goat leathers, these jackets are designed to develop character and soften over time.

Material Specifications & Technology

Primary Materials: Most contemporary models utilize 100% lamb leather for its soft, luxe feel or goat leather for enhanced durability. Some technical "mixed media" styles incorporate high-performance combo materials like cotton, polyester, and elastane for flexibility.
Lining Technology: Interior linings often consist of a polyester and elastane blend (typically 95%/5%) to provide a smooth "slip" over other clothing while allowing for movement. Technical shells used in some models are engineered for weather performance.
Hardware & Design: High-quality metallic zippers, press-stud lapel fastenings, and branded hood details are standard. Functional features often include zippered expansion cuffs for mobility and removable hooded "dickies" for versatile styling.
