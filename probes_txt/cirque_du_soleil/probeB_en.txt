Query: Cirque du Soleil show details specs pricing tickets

For 2026, Cirque du Soleil's flagship touring production is ECHO, appearing in major cities like Montreal and Dallas. Other major resident and touring shows like LUZIA, OVO, and Las Vegas classics continue their runs with varying specs and pricing.

Current Featured Show: ECHO

Theme: A story about evolution and the symbiotic relationship between humans, the animal kingdom, and the natural world, centered around a massive 23-foot cube.
Show Duration: 125 minutes, including a 25-minute intermission.
Age Requirement: Family-friendly and recommended for all ages; children 2 and older require their own ticket.

Ticket Pricing:
Standard: Starting from $59 CAD up to $89 CAD depending on the date and time.
Premium/VIP: Can exceed $300 CAD during peak times.

Locations (2026):
Dallas, TX: April 9 – May 3 at Lone Star Park.
Montreal, QC: May 21 – June 28 at the Old Port.

Ticket & Booking Details

Official Box Office: Tickets can be purchased directly via the Cirque du Soleil Official Site or by phone at 1-877-924-7783.
Group Rates: Discounts are typically available for groups of 10 or more people.
Accessibility: Most Big Top venues are fully wheelchair accessible; specialized "ADA" or reduced mobility tickets can be selected during the online checkout process.
Payment: On-site box offices generally accept cash, credit, and debit, while online bookings require a credit card or digital payment.

Venues & Amenities

Big Top Experience: Touring shows like ECHO and LUZIA are presented under the "Grand Chapiteau" (Big Top). These venues include concession tents for snacks and merchandise but typically do not offer full-service restaurants or coat checks.
Photography: Non-flash photography is generally permitted before the show and during intermission.
